My Approach

How I turn research into meaningful design

My process begins with understanding real people, then translating insights into clear, accessible, and usable digital experiences.

01

Understand

I begin by learning about users, their goals, frustrations, behaviours, and real-life context.

02

Define

I identify the core problem, key user needs, and design opportunities before jumping into solutions.

03

Design

I create flows, wireframes, and interfaces that reduce friction and make tasks easier to complete.

04

Test

I test ideas with users, refine interactions, and improve clarity, accessibility, and usability.
