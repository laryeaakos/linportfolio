Interactive UX Case Study

AI Observability Investigation Assistant

This is a click-through experience designed to help users investigate abnormal AI system behaviour step by step, instead of facing a dense dashboard all at once.

01 / Scenario Introduction
Interactive prototype
Exit case study

Design intention: The interaction is structured around three user questions: What happened? What does it mean? What should I do next? Each screen reveals only the information needed at that moment.
