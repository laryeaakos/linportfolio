Product Design Case Study

Tasky Smart Family Noticeboard

An accessible family noticeboard for everyday coordination. Tasky helps families see what matters now, reduce mental overload, and manage routines with less friction.

Role Product Designer
Focus Family coordination
Methods Research, prototyping
Theme Accessibility
The Problem

Family organisation was not failing because families lacked calendars

Families were already using calendars, notes, reminders, and memory. The real challenge was that information was scattered, plans changed quickly, and the mental effort of keeping family life together became too heavy.

01

Mental overload

Too much responsibility lived in one person’s head.

02

Plan changes

Routines shifted quickly and were easy to miss.

03

Everything overlaps

School, home, work, and family tasks competed for attention.

04

Home and work collide

Parents needed clearer information at the right moment.

Research Insight

The research challenged my first assumption

I initially thought families needed one better calendar. The research pointed to a different problem: families needed a shared, calm way to see what matters now.

Initial assumption

Families need one calendar that combines everything.

Research insight

Mental load and unexpected changes caused more stress than simply having too many tools.

Design Direction

The solution did not need to become a better calendar

Instead, Tasky became a shared family noticeboard — a calm home display that highlights the most relevant information at the right time.

01

One view

A shared home display helps families see what matters without opening multiple apps.

02

One task at a time

The interface reduces pressure by focusing attention on the next important action.

03

Voice support

Voice interaction helps Tasky feel more accessible and less dependent on touch.

Key takeaway

Tasky taught me that reducing cognitive load is not only about removing information. It is about giving families the right information, at the right priority, at the right moment.
