Featured Case Study

Elysian Fields Attendance Kiosk

A research-led RFID kiosk concept designed to reduce paper-based attendance friction and make staff check-in easier, clearer, and more accessible.

The Problem

Teachers and staff relied on a paper attendance book, which led to delayed entries, forgotten check-ins, and unreliable attendance records during busy mornings.

Discuss this project →

Key Insight

The solution was not just a better digital logbook. It was moving the check-in touchpoint into the teacher’s natural entry path using an RFID kiosk.
