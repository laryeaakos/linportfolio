Featured Case Study

Elysian Fields Attendance Kiosk

I researched an existing staff attendance process and discovered that the central problem was not resistance to the system, but delayed, memory-based logging. The resulting solution moved away from a mobile concept toward an RFID-enabled shared kiosk placed in the natural arrival path.

✓ Research changed the original product direction.
✓ RFID identification reduced interaction effort.
✓ Accessibility was considered in the physical and digital experience.
